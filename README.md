## Executive Summary & Brand Profile
**Tat by Chrissy** is an artistic, multi-service body art, nail care, and skincare lifestyle studio operating in Gauteng, South Africa. Designed as a modern, edgy, and accessible visual platform, the website serves as a primary client lead-generation channel, service directory, and booking portal.

The platform directly addresses the needs of clients seeking custom body art, certified hygienic piercings, aesthetic nail care, and specialized post-procedure healing products.

---

## Business Goals and Strategic Objectives
1. **Digital Storefront Presence:** Establish a cohesive, high-end online presence reflecting the brand’s artistic standard and commitment to sterility.
2. **Multi-Location Visibility:** Clearly present physical studio information, operating hours, and contact channels for both the **Rosebank Main Studio** and **Sandton Express Lounge**.
3. **Interactive Client Acquisition:** Streamline client intake and consultation requests through a structured online enquiry interface.
4. **Service & Product Education:** Provide explicit information regarding service categories (tattoos, piercings, nails) and aftercare skincare maintenance to build client trust.

---

## User Experience (UX) & Design Rationale
* **Target Audience:** Modern young adults, body art enthusiasts, and individuals prioritizing safe, professional, and hygienic beauty environments.
* **Aesthetic Direction:** Modern, dark-themed, artistic aesthetic with clear typography and minimalist layouts to highlight artwork and service details.
* **Navigation Architecture:** A consistent, fixed header navigation bar across all pages ensures low cognitive load and intuitive movement across the platform.
* **Responsive Layout Strategy:** Clean, semantic HTML sectioning structured for responsive adaptation across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
