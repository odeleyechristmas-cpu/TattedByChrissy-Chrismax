About Tat by Chrissy

Combining creative self-expression with modern skincare and beauty standards.

Our History

Founded by Chrissy, Tat by Chrissy began as a passion project focused on artistic body art. Over time, it grew into a full-service lounge combining custom tattooing, precision piercings, nail artistry, and specialized body aftercare products under one roof.

Our studio is built around individuality, quality care, and a welcoming experience where every client feels confident, safe, and inspired.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To deliver safe, hygienic, and artistic body care solutions that empower clients to express their individuality with confidence.

Vision

To become a trusted lifestyle brand known for exceptional artistic quality, sterile hygiene standards, and comprehensive client care.

Values

Creative expression, clean practices, comfort, and genuine support for every journey from consultation to aftercare.
